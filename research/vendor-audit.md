# Agent / LLM observability vendor audit

**As-of date:** 2026-09-01  
**Scope:** evidence available in the vendors' official documentation, official API references, and official source repositories as of the date above.

## Question and decision rule

This audit asks whether an agent-observability or LLM-observability product has **CFO-facing unit economics**, not merely LLM telemetry. A vendor is counted as CFO-facing only when official evidence establishes **all three** of the following:

1. **Cost per completed case/workflow:** provider/model cost is attached to an explicitly completed business case or workflow unit. A trace, request, session, span, or token total by itself is not a completed unit; an average or total without a documented completion denominator does not pass.
2. **Chargeback/showback/allocation:** official product documentation explicitly describes showback, chargeback, or allocation to an accountable team, business unit, product, customer, or cost center. A free-form tag, metadata field, or group-by dimension alone is partial evidence, not a pass.
3. **Waste attribution:** official evidence attributes cost or usage to **each** of retries, failed work, abandoned work, and unattributed work (or provides an equivalent explicit waste taxonomy). Error counts or retry controls without cost attribution do not pass.

`Yes` means the reviewed primary source explicitly supports the criterion. `Partial` means the source supports a neighboring capability but not the strict criterion. `Unknown` means the reviewed primary sources are insufficient; this is deliberately not an inference of absence. No claim below relies on generic “cost tracking” language alone.

## Methodology and vendor selection

I reviewed product documentation and API/source material owned by each vendor, using stable URLs and excluding analyst reports, third-party comparisons, and vendor-neutral summaries. For each product I looked specifically for: completed-unit semantics, cost roll-up boundaries, organizational allocation language, and cost-bearing retry/error/abandonment/unattributed states. I separately recorded engineer-facing traces, token counts, latency, status/error views, and raw spend dashboards so they are not mistaken for finance controls.

The sample balances four prominent open-source/community projects (Langfuse, Helicone, OpenLLMetry/Traceloop, and Arize Phoenix) with six commercial or major observability products (LangSmith, Datadog, New Relic, Braintrust, Honeycomb, and Dynatrace). OSS status is linked to each project's official repository; commercial status is based on the vendor product documentation, not a market-size claim.

### Strict CFO-facing count

**0 of 10 vendors pass the strict threshold (all three criteria = Yes).** Several products have useful building blocks—trace-level or request-level estimated cost, metadata dimensions, error views, or separate cloud-cost allocation—but the reviewed official evidence does not establish the complete combination of completed-unit economics, organizational chargeback/showback, and four-class waste attribution. This count intentionally treats missing evidence as `Unknown` rather than inferring a negative capability.

## Vendor evidence

### Langfuse

- **Cost evidence:** Langfuse records usage and USD cost primarily on `generation` and `embedding` observations, with provider-ingested or model-pricing-inferred values. Its metrics documentation lists cost/token/latency metrics and dimensions such as user, session, feature, and tags. This is a strong engineer-facing cost-observation layer, but the sources do not define a completed case or workflow completion denominator. [Token & Cost Tracking](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/features/token-and-cost-tracking), [Metrics Overview](https://langfuse.com/docs/metrics/overview)
- **Allocation evidence:** User/session/tag dimensions can segment analysis, but the reviewed pages do not say that Langfuse performs chargeback, showback, or cost-center allocation. Therefore allocation is `Partial`, not `Yes`. [Metrics Overview](https://langfuse.com/docs/metrics/overview), [Tags](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/features/tags), [Users](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/features/users)
- **Waste evidence:** Sessions group related traces, but the reviewed sources do not establish a cost-waste taxonomy covering retries, failed, abandoned, and unattributed work. Status or trace grouping alone is not treated as waste attribution. [Sessions](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/features/sessions), [Core Data Model](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/data-model)
- **Project type:** The official repository identifies Langfuse as source-available/open source software. [Langfuse repository](https://github.com/langfuse/langfuse)

### Helicone

- **Cost evidence:** Helicone documents request-level cost and token usage, custom properties for organization/feature/user segmentation, and HQL queries that sum cost. The request/session/property model does not establish a completed-case state or denominator. [Cost Tracking](https://docs.helicone.ai/guides/cookbooks/cost-tracking), [Custom Properties](https://docs.helicone.ai/features/advanced-usage/custom-properties), [HQL](https://docs.helicone.ai/features/hql)
- **Allocation evidence:** Organization and other custom properties support spend segmentation, but the reviewed documentation does not explicitly describe chargeback, showback, or allocation to a cost center. This remains `Partial`. [Custom Properties](https://docs.helicone.ai/features/advanced-usage/custom-properties), [Cost Tracking](https://docs.helicone.ai/guides/cookbooks/cost-tracking)
- **Waste evidence:** Helicone documents HTTP error filtering and retries for selected status codes. That proves operational error/retry observability, not cost attribution across retry, failed, abandoned, and unattributed work; the latter is `Partial` only. [Alerts](https://docs.helicone.ai/features/alerts), [Retries](https://docs.helicone.ai/features/advanced-usage/retries), [Request query API](https://docs.helicone.ai/rest/request/post-v1requestquery-clickhouse)
- **Project type:** The official repository is the Helicone OSS LLM observability project. [Helicone repository](https://github.com/Helicone/helicone)

- **Allocation evidence:** Association properties are useful grouping keys, but reviewed API/docs pages do not explicitly provide showback, chargeback, or allocation rules. `Partial`. [Costs by association property API](https://www.traceloop.com/docs/api-reference/costs/property_costs)
- **Waste evidence:** The reviewed sources do not establish retry, failed, abandoned, and unattributed cost classes. `Unknown`. [Costs by association property API](https://www.traceloop.com/docs/api-reference/costs/property_costs), [OpenLLMetry introduction](https://traceloop.com/docs/openllmetry/introduction)

## Conservative G0 recommendation

**Do not select any audited vendor as evidence that G0 already has CFO-grade unit economics.** Treat the market as providing observability primitives: estimated provider cost, token/latency/error traces, request or session rollups, metadata dimensions, and (in some suites) separate cloud-cost allocation. None of the ten reviewed products has official evidence meeting the strict all-three contract as of 2026-09-01. G0 should therefore preserve explicit business-unit identity and completion/outcome state, and retain retry/failed/abandoned/unattributed cost-bearing attempts, rather than assuming a vendor trace or spend dashboard supplies those controls.

This recommendation is deliberately conservative: it is a decision about documented evidence, not a claim that vendors cannot be extended or configured to approximate the missing controls.
